Google Search Ranking & Discoverability Capstone

Problem: A manual content-review process cannot inspect every possible CTR opportunity.

Approach: I compared a transparent February-only baseline with a leakage-aware Random Forest, validating on 12 entirely unseen pseudonymized clients.

Outcome: Model Precision@20 reached 0.90 versus 0.25 for the baseline. The April scoring workflow organized 107,144 eligible items into REVIEW, INVESTIGATE and MONITOR groups.

Boundary: The system provides observational decision support. It does not automatically edit pages or establish causal impact.
