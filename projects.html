Google Search Ranking & Discoverability Capstone

Problem: A manual content-review process cannot inspect every possible CTR opportunity.

Outcome: Model Precision@20 reached 0.90 versus 0.25 for the baseline. The April workflow organized 107,144 eligible items into REVIEW, INVESTIGATE and MONITOR groups.

Boundary: Observational decision support only—it does not automatically edit pages or establish causal impact.
