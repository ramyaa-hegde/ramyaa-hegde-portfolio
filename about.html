About Ramya Hegde

From Operations to AI/ML - the long version.

A professional transition built on business context, formal data science foundations, and applied technical work.

The Story

I started my career in operations - first at Reliance Jio, then Dunzo, and now as a Senior Operations Analyst at First Advantage. Along the way, I kept running into the same pattern: the biggest wins didn't come from working harder inside a process. They came from understanding the data behind the process well enough to fix it at the root.

That is what pulled me toward AI/ML. I'm now pursuing a BSc in Data Science & Analytics at Jain University and completing hands-on machine-learning work through the FlyRank AI/ML Engineering Internship - building real systems, not only studying theory.
