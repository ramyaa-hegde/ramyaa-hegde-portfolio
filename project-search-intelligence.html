Search Intelligence Engine & Content Opportunity Scoring

A public-safe decision-support workflow for prioritizing content items for manual CTR review.

01

The problem

The project tests whether February public-safe search and engagement signals can rank eligible content items likely to have below-benchmark CTR in March more effectively than a transparent February-only baseline.

02

Data and validation

The final modelling frame contained 73,481 eligible client-content observations across 33 pseudonymized clients. Twenty-one clients were used for training and 12 were held out, with zero client overlap.

03

Model and results

A Random Forest achieved Precision@20 of 0.900 versus 0.250 for the transparent baseline. Average Precision was 0.584 versus 0.339, and ROC-AUC was 0.730 versus 0.486 on the held-out population.

04

Operational output

Model scores were translated into human-review action categories with monitoring and retraining triggers. Row-level recommendations were not published; only public-safe aggregate evidence was exported.

05

Responsible interpretation

The results support prioritization for the defined proxy label and validation population. They do not prove that an edit will cause more clicks or reveal how a private search algorithm works.
